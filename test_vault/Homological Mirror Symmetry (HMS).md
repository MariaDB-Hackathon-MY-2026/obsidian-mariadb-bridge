Originally inspired by **String Theory**, HMS suggests that two different types of geometric spaces—**Symplectic** and **Complex**—are actually equivalent at a deep algebraic level (Chan, 2017).

### Core Research Focus

- **The Kontsevich Conjecture:** Proposed in 1994, it asserts that the _derived Fukaya category_ of a symplectic manifold $X$ is equivalent to the _derived category of coherent sheaves_ on its complex mirror $\check{X}$ (Chan, 2017).
    
- **SYZ Fibrations:** The Strominger-Yau-Zaslow (SYZ) conjecture provides the geometric mechanism for this symmetry, suggesting mirror manifolds admit dual Lagrangian torus fibrations (Chan, 2017).
    

### Modern Applications (2025-2026)

- **Quantum Cohomology:** Research into how "instanton corrections" and holomorphic disks bounded by fibers can correct the gluing of complex structures in mirror pairs (Chan, 2017).
    
- **Batyrev’s Mirror:** Detailed isomorphism of variations of integral Hodge structures for Calabi-Yau hypersurfaces (Iritani, 2011).
    
- **Formalization in Lean 4:** There is a growing movement to formalize complex proofs in HMS and TDA using **Agentic AI** and interactive theorem provers like **Lean 4** to ensure absolute logical consistency (University of Cambridge, 2026).
